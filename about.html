The Story Behind TheHGTech
I built TheHGTech because the signal-to-noise ratio in cybersecurity content had become unbearable. Too many headlines, too few actionable takeaways. Too much vendor marketing dressed as research. This is my answer to that problem — a personal project that gives back to the security community with research-grade content I'd actually want to read myself. No affiliate deals, no sponsored rankings, no filler. Just the real picture, clearly explained.
Content Philosophy
Every piece of content here is written with practitioners in mind — people who need to act on information, not just consume it. I aim to write at the depth level of a paid threat intelligence report, without the enterprise price tag.

I label analysis clearly, cite primary sources, and never publish numbers I can't verify. Accuracy is non-negotiable — even when the truth is less dramatic than the headline.

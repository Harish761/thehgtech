Integrity & Engineering Charter

Editorial Methodology & Research Standards

Every analysis published on TheHGTech follows deterministic technical standards. We reject marketing hype and vendor-funded reviews in favor of independent, reproducible research.

Independent Technical Research

Zero sponsored placements or vendor pay-to-play content. All architectural dissections, packet captures, decompiled binaries, and kernel analysis are produced independently in our labs.

Deterministic Verification & PoC Testing

We do not aggregate third-party claims without reproduction. Proof-of-concept exploits, CVSS metric models, and attack trees are tested in isolated environments before publication.

Defensive Guidance & Actionable Engineering

Security analysis is incomplete without defensive countermeasures. We accompany technical dissections with concrete Sigma rules, YARA hunting signatures, KQL queries, and architectural hardening playbooks.

Empirical Blast-Radius Triage

Rather than relying solely on generic CVSS scores, we evaluate reachability, exploit prerequisites, kernel filter chaining, and identity exposure to determine real-world enterprise operational risk.

Publication Policy & Transparency

Editorial Standards & Publishing Practices

TheHGTech is an independent cybersecurity publication and threat-intelligence resource maintained by Harish G. This page documents how articles, guides, and automated intelligence dashboards on this site are authored, sourced, updated, and corrected.

Editorial Lead: Harish G Funding Model: Independent (no pay-to-play vendor rankings) Corrections & Security Contact: harish@thehgtech.com

1. Editorial Independence

TheHGTech operates as an independent practitioner-run project. Tool comparisons, technical guides, and vulnerability analyses are written without vendor sponsorship, paid placement, or affiliate commissions.

When vendor products, CVEs, or security incidents are covered, technical details are anchored to publicly available advisories, vendor documentation, or primary security disclosures whenever available.

2. Human-Authored Content vs. Automated Feeds

TheHGTech clearly separates editorial writing from automated telemetry:

  • Research Articles & Security Guides: Authored and edited under bylines (such as Harish G) to explain vulnerabilities, breach mechanics, defensive engineering controls, and compliance frameworks.
  • Automated Intelligence Dashboards: Pages including Threat Intelligence, CVE Tracker, Ransomware Tracker, and Hacker Chatter are updated by scheduled GitHub Actions pipelines directly from public security feeds.
  • Daily News Shorts & Summaries: Short-form daily security summaries are generated with automated aggregation pipelines and clearly presented separately from long-form technical monographs.

3. Intelligence Sources & Verification Scope

Automated dashboards ingest data exclusively from public, documented sources rather than private or direct dark-web access:

  • Vulnerability & Exploit Data: CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV), NIST National Vulnerability Database (NVD), and FIRST EPSS probability scores.
  • Indicator Feeds: OpenPhish, Spamhaus DROP, CINS Army, Blocklist.de, and the abuse.ch feed family (URLhaus, ThreatFox, MalwareBazaar, Feodo Tracker, SSL Blacklist).
  • Ransomware & OSINT Claims: Public OSINT trackers such as Ransomware.live cross-referenced against Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) public breach metadata. Threat actor leak-site claims are explicitly presented as unverified OSINT disclosures unless independently confirmed.

4. Safe Indicator Handling & Privacy

  • Defanged Indicators: Malicious URLs and IP addresses displayed on Threat Intelligence views are defanged (for example, hxxps:// and [.]) to reduce the risk of accidental clicks.
  • No Tor Hosting or Stolen Data Mirrors: TheHGTech does not proxy .onion sites and never hosts or links to stolen breach datasets.
  • Client-Side GRC Tooling: The ISO 27001 GRC Assessment tool executes locally in the browser with zero client-side analytics scripts, self-hosted export libraries, and fragment-based (#s=) share links. See our Privacy Policy for site-wide details.

5. Corrections, Updates & Security Disclosure

Cybersecurity disclosures evolve rapidly as vendors release patches, revise CVE scopes, or publish post-incident findings. When factual errors, broken citations, or outdated remediation guidance are identified, pages are updated directly in the public Git repository so changes remain inspectable.

To request a correction, flag an inaccurate source attribution, or report a security issue affecting thehgtech.com, please email harish@thehgtech.com or consult /.well-known/security.txt. You can also find background and contact links on our About & Contact page.

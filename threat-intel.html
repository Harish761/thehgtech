Methodology & Data Sources

TheHGTech aggregates open-source threat intelligence from authoritative global feeds. Our pipeline normalizes, deduplicates, and enriches data with campaign detection to deliver actionable, noise-free telemetry.

9+ Trusted Sensor Feeds

We ingest structured intelligence from OpenPhish, URLhaus, ThreatFox, Malware Bazaar, Spamhaus DROP, CINS Army, Blocklist.de, Feodo Tracker, and SSL Blacklist every 4 hours.

Safe Defanged Storage

All indicators are systematically defanged to prevent accidental navigation while maintaining full fidelity. Outbound analysis links are refanged securely in memory on demand.

7-Day Rolling Horizon

Stale indicators are automatically pruned after 7 days, maintaining a high-signal, relevant threat landscape with minimal operational bloat.

ISO 27001 Governance Requirement 9.x: Executive Management Review

Domain: Management System Requirements (Governance Core)

Auditor Question

Is management reviewing the ISMS at planned intervals — with documented minutes covering required inputs (audit results, risk treatment status, objective progress) and resulting decisions and actions?

Objective

Ensure executive accountability is exercised periodically — not just at certification time — keeping the ISMS aligned to evolving business strategy and risk landscape.

Evidence Required

Provide signed Management Review Meeting Minutes from the past 12 months, showing the standard required inputs were discussed and that outputs (decisions, resource allocations) were formally recorded.

Expert Rationale

Management Review is the top-level feedback loop of the ISMS. Without it, executives are managing security blindly. Auditors treat missing or superficial review minutes as a direct indicator of weak governance.

Remediation Advice

Schedule a formal ISMS Management Review at least annually (ideally twice yearly). Use a structured agenda covering all ISO 27001-required inputs. Document outcomes, decisions, and action items with owners. Store signed minutes in the ISMS document repository.

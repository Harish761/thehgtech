ISO 27001 Storage Media

Domain: Physical Controls

Auditor Question

Are storage media (USB drives, backup tapes) managed throughout their lifecycle of acquisition, use, transportation, and disposal?

Objective

Protect against data leaks originating from lost or improperly disposed of physical storage devices.

Evidence Required

Provide a media inventory log and the 'Secure Media Disposal' certificate from a professional shredding vendor.

Expert Rationale

One forgotten USB stick in a parking lot can lead to a 'Rubber Ducky' attack. One hard drive sold on eBay without being wiped can lead to a total data breach. Media management is about controlling every bit of 'removable' data.

Remediation Advice

Ban the use of unencrypted USB drives. Require that all decommissioned hard drives are physically shredded or degaussed by an authorized 3rd party vendor.

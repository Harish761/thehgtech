ISO 27001 Addressing Information Security within Supplier Agreements

Domain: Organizational Controls

Auditor Question

Are rigorous information security requirements contractually embedded in all agreements with third-party suppliers?

Objective

Ensure legal enforcement of security standards across the vendor ecosystem.

Evidence Required

Provide an SLA or vendor contract highlighting specific data breach notification clauses and security requirements.

Expert Rationale

A 'handshake agreement' won't help you after a breach. You need clear, legally binding clauses that force your vendors to notify you immediately if your customers' data is exposed.

Remediation Advice

Work with legal counsel to draft a standard 'Data Processing Agreement' (DPA) requiring vendors to report any breaches to you within 48-72 hours.

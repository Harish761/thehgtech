ISO 27001 Response to Information Security Incidents

Domain: Organizational Controls

Auditor Question

Are incidents actively responded to in accordance with the documented procedures in the Incident Response Plan?

Objective

Limit the blast radius and damage caused by a cyber attack through coordinated, pre-planned actions.

Evidence Required

Provide a post-incident report from a recent (or simulated) event showing containment, eradication, and communication timelines.

Expert Rationale

Execution beats planning. It doesn't matter how good your PDF plan is if your sysadmin doesn't know which server to pull the plug on during an attack. Regular 'Tabletop Exercises' turn theory into muscle memory.

Remediation Advice

Run an annual tabletop simulation encompassing ransomware scenarios to train executives and IT staff on rapid response workflows.

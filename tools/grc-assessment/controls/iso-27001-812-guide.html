ISO 27001 Data Leakage Prevention

Domain: Technological Controls

Auditor Question

Are technical data leakage prevention (DLP) measures applied to systems that process, store, or transmit sensitive information?

Objective

Automatically detect and block the unauthorized exfiltration of sensitive organizational data.

Evidence Required

Provide a DLP alert report showing the detection or blocking of a sensitive pattern (e.g., SSN, Credit Card) being sent via email.

Expert Rationale

Accidents happen. DLP is the 'Safety Net' that stops an employee from accidentally emailing a sensitive spreadsheet to the wrong person. It watches for patterns like credit card numbers and stops the data before it leaves the building.

Remediation Advice

Enable DLP features in your email suite (Google Workspace/M365) to block the sending of documents containing unencrypted credit card numbers or PII.

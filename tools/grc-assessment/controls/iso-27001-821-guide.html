ISO 27001 Security of Network Services

Domain: Technological Controls

Auditor Question

Are security mechanisms, service levels, and management requirements of all network services (VPNs, DNS, etc.) identified and monitored?

Objective

Ensure that 'Invisible' core services like DNS aren't hijacked to redirect users to malicious sites.

Evidence Required

Provide the service contract for your managed DNS or VPN provider, highlighting the security requirements.

Expert Rationale

DNS and VPNs are the 'Nervous System' of your network. If an attacker hijacks your DNS, they can make 'gmail.com' point to their own fake login page. Securing these core services is 'Critical Infrastructure' defense.

Remediation Advice

Utilize DNSSEC to prevent DNS hijacking. Enforce 'Strong Encryption' (IKEv2/TLS 1.3) for all corporate VPN tunnel connections.

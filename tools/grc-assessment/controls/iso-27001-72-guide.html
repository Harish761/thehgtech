ISO 27001 Physical Entry Controls

Domain: Physical Controls

Auditor Question

Are secure areas protected by appropriate entry controls to ensure that only authorized personnel are allowed access?

Objective

Verify the identity of every individual entering secure zones to maintain an audit trail of physical presence.

Evidence Required

Provide a report from the electronic badge system showing recent entry logs or a physical visitor sign-in sheet.

Expert Rationale

Identity verification shouldn't stop at the login screen. Physical entry controls (badges/biometrics) ensure that you know exactly who was in the office when a piece of hardware goes missing or a 'rubber ducky' is plugged into a workstation.

Remediation Advice

Deploy an electronic badge system for all office entry points. Mandate that visitors are escorted by an employee at all times.

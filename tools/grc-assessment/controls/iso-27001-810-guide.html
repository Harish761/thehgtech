ISO 27001 Information Deletion

Domain: Technological Controls

Auditor Question

Is information stored in information systems and devices deleted or securely overwritten when no longer required?

Objective

Minimize the 'Toxic Data' footprint by removing old, sensitive data that is no longer needed for business or legal reasons.

Evidence Required

Provide evidence of an automated data deletion script or policy sign-off for retiring old databases.

Expert Rationale

Old data is a liability, not an asset. If you are hacked, you will be fined for every record stolen. if you delete old data you no longer need, that data can't be stolen. It's the ultimate 'Data Minimization' strategy.

Remediation Advice

Establish an automated data lifecycle policy that permanently deletes customer records 7 years after account termination.

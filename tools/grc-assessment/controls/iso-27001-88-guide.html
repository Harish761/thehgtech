ISO 27001 Management of Technical Vulnerabilities

Domain: Technological Controls

Auditor Question

Are security vulnerabilities actively identified, evaluated, and remediated in a timely manner (Scanning & Patching)?

Objective

Close the technical 'windows of opportunity' that attackers use to gain entry to your systems.

Evidence Required

Provide a sample vulnerability scan report from tools like Nessus or Qualys, and a corresponding 'Closed' ticket showing a critical patch was applied.

Expert Rationale

**Risk:** Exploitation of known technical weaknesses.
**Impact:** Hackers don't always find new exploits; they mostly use the ones you haven't patched yet.
**Guidance:** Vulnerability management is about 'Patching the Holes' before the rain starts. If you don't patch, you're essentially leaving your windows unlocked.

Remediation Advice

Conduct monthly internal and external vulnerability scans. Establish a SLA requiring 'Critical' vulnerabilities to be patched within 72 hours.

ISO 27001 Terms and Conditions of Employment

Domain: People Controls

Auditor Question

Do employment agreements explicitly state the employee's legal responsibilities for protecting the organization's confidential data?

Objective

Establish a legally binding obligation for personnel to protect company assets both during and after their tenure.

Evidence Required

Provide a standard template employment contract containing an NDA and an information security responsibility clause.

Expert Rationale

If a security expectation isn't in the contract, it's very hard to enforce in court. Defining responsibilities 'by law' protects the company's IP if an employee decides to leak data to a competitor.

Remediation Advice

Require all new hires and contractors to sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) on Day 1 before receiving access to any internal IT systems.

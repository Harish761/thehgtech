ISO 27001 Information Access Restriction

Domain: Technological Controls

Auditor Question

Is access to information and application system functions restricted in accordance with the established topic-specific policy on access control?

Objective

Enforce the Principle of Least Privilege at the application and data layer.

Evidence Required

Provide a screenshot of application-level permissions showing that an HR user cannot view Engineering source code repositories.

Expert Rationale

Internal partitioning is key. Access restriction ensures that if your marketing intern's account is hacked, the attacker can't download your patent portfolio or payroll data. It's about containing the 'Blast Radius' of a compromise.

Remediation Advice

Review all folder and application permissions. Remove 'Everyone' or 'Authenticated Users' from sensitive data shares and replace with specific AD groups.

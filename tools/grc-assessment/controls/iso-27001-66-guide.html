ISO 27001 Confidentiality or Non-disclosure Agreements

Domain: People Controls

Auditor Question

Are universally robust NDAs executed outlining the exact definitions and handling instructions for confidential information?

Objective

Provide strong legal recourse in the event of unauthorized organizational data leakage.

Evidence Required

Provide a copy of the active corporate NDA template. Highlight definitions of what constitutes 'Confidential Information'.

Expert Rationale

An NDA is your baseline defensive shield. It defines what is 'secret' and sets the penalty for telling those secrets. Without it, your trade secrets are legally 'public domain.'

Remediation Advice

Audit current contractor agreements to ensure NDAs natively cover modern data scopes (like source code, customer DBs, and API documentation).

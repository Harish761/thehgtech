ISO 27001 Governance Requirement 4.x: Business Context & Organization

Domain: Management System Requirements (Governance Core)

Auditor Question

Has the organisation formally documented the internal and external issues relevant to its ISMS, and identified the needs and expectations of interested parties (regulators, customers, partners)?

Objective

Establish a clear, formally scoped ISMS that is aligned to business context and stakeholder expectations — the foundation for all risk-based decisions.

Evidence Required

Provide the documented Context Analysis (or equivalent PESTLE/stakeholder analysis) showing the organisation's ISMS boundary definition and list of interested parties with their requirements.

Expert Rationale

**Risk:** Operating an ISMS without understanding its environment.
**Impact:** Controls are misaligned to actual threats, causing over-investment in irrelevant areas and blind spots in critical ones.
**Guidance:** Context is the lens through which all risk decisions are made. Without it, your ISMS is built on assumptions, not facts.

Remediation Advice

Conduct a formal context workshop to document internal factors (culture, capabilities) and external factors (legal landscape, market). Capture interested party requirements in an ISMS Scope document signed by executive leadership.

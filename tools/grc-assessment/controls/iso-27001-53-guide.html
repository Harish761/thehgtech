ISO 27001 Segregation of Duties

Domain: Organizational Controls

Auditor Question

Are conflicting duties (e.g., development vs. production deployment) separated to prevent unauthorized changes or fraud?

Objective

Mitigate the risk of intentional fraud or accidental catastrophic errors by requiring a two-person rule for critical actions.

Evidence Required

Provide an access matrix or Git workflow documentation showing how developers are restricted from pushing directly to production without review.

Expert Rationale

**Risk:** Internal fraud or catastrophic accidental errors.
**Impact:** A single compromised or disgruntled employee could delete your entire production environment.
**Guidance:** Enforce 'Two-Person' rules (like PR reviews) for all critical changes to prevent a 'single point of human failure' from destroying the business.

Remediation Advice

Enforce branch protection rules in GitHub/GitLab requiring at least one approving review before merging to main. Revoke direct production access for standard developers.

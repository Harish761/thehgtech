ISO 27001 Privacy and Protection of PII

Domain: Organizational Controls

Auditor Question

Are stringent processes in place to ensure the privacy and protection of Personally Identifiable Information (PII) according to relevant legislation?

Objective

Safeguard user privacy and prevent costly class-action lawsuits or fines under frameworks like GDPR or CCPA.

Evidence Required

Provide the external Privacy Policy and the internal procedures for handling Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs).

Expert Rationale

Privacy is a civil right. If you lose a customer's credit card, it's bad. If you lose their home address, health data, or social security number (PII), it's a lifespan-altering event for the victim and a business-ending event for the company.

Remediation Advice

Implement data mapping to understand exactly where PII flows. Build a standardized, trackable process for honoring user deletion requests within 30 days.
